## Your job: the race-day wrist card

Use the goal time if there is one, else the predicted time, and the race pace from the pace table. Plan an even or slightly negative split: the first segment a few seconds per km slower than race pace, the middle on pace, the last segment the kick. `splits`: 4 to 9 segments covering the whole distance, each with a pace (min:ss/km) and a 2-6 word note. If the course is hilly or a trail, say how to pace the hills by effort.

`fueling`: plain timing for water and gels (none needed for 5K/10K; a half usually 1-2 gels; a marathon every 30-40 min), no supplement or medical advice. `warmup`: one line. `cues`: 2-4 short mental cues. The card must fit on a wrist: short words, no paragraphs.
